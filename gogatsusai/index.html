Robot Demonstrasions at 第98回五月祭 (May Festival)

2025

Overview

GVLAB (PI: Professor Gentiane Venture) participated in the May Festival at the University of Tokyo on May 24th and 25th, 2025.

At our booth, the participants were able to play cards with a robot, collaborate with a robot in a block-building time challenge, and experience several other exciting robot demonstrations. It’s a fun and interactive way to get a glimpse of cutting-edge robotics and human-robot interaction.

I led the overall planning and management of the event, including developing our main attraction: a card-playing robot.
