About

Spatial Production Engineer at teamLab, designing and developing the technical components behind unique digital art experiences, with a focus on hardware, electrical circuits, and software integration.
I received my B.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering from Kyushu University in 2024 under the supervision of Dr. Ayato Kanada, and my M.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering from The University of Tokyo in 2026 under the supervision of Dr. Gentiane Venture. I was also a visiting scholar at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in Fall 2025 under the supervision of Dr. Joohyung Kim .
During my studies, I worked on various areas of real-world Human-Robot Interaction research, including adaptive navigation, expressive motion for manipulators, and full-body motion retargeting for bipedal robots.
I aspire to create engineering solutions that broaden people’s perspectives and deliver memorable experiences.
